Strategic Websites

SeaducedExperience

Tourist website designed to present and sell private boat experiences, focusing on clarity, premium aesthetics and conversion, optimized for simple and responsive navigation on all devices.

Management

Online Booking System for Businesses

Complete web platform that allows any appointment-based business to manage its schedule autonomously. Clients can book online at any time, choosing the service, date and available time slot, and receive an email confirmation with the option to cancel up to 24 hours in advance.

Includes an admin panel to configure services, pricing and hours, block days or time slots, and view all appointments for the day. Features billing statistics, most in-demand services, and analysis of peak days and hours. Automatic email reminders and full configuration from the panel.

Automated Ordering System

Web system designed to structure and automate orders from WhatsApp and Instagram, eliminating manual management and organizing operations into a clear and centralized flow.

Includes digital catalog, automatic order capture, real-time administrative panel and control of products, sales and customers, reducing errors and improving operational efficiency.

Rebels Kite School

Web management system developed for surf and kite schools, designed to organize classes, instructors, students, payments and billing through an interactive calendar, status control and role-differentiated panels.
